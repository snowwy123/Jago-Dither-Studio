# A few things to try

No perfect settings here. Start small, move a slider, and see what happens.

## Two patterns on one page

Choose a main print style, then open **Tone duet**. The main style stays in darker areas; the second pattern appears in lighter ones. Move the meeting point and soften the blend. Try diamonds with diagonal lines, or a soft scatter with halftone dots.

## Make a little tile

Choose **Your little tile** in Print and open its editor. Select a value, then click a square to paint it. The 4 × 4 tile repeats across the image. Lower values put down ink sooner; higher values leave more paper.

Use Pattern size and angle to change how it sits on the page. Save a recipe to keep or share it. Try painting repeated values instead of using every number once: it makes bolder, less regular marks.

## Colour by brightness

In Ink, enable a two- or three-colour **Gradient map**. Choose colours for shadows, midtones and highlights. These become the printing inks while the map is on; the normal ink shelf is temporarily inactive.

Brightness and Midtones in Print still affect where colours appear. Reverse inks swaps the final colours without changing the shape of the marks. Turning it off restores the original assignment.

## Lettering with a bit of colour

Add a text layer, then choose a linear or radial fill under **Fill with a little colour**. Change the two colours and the angle. These colours belong to the original composition; the dither palette or gradient map controls the final printed colours.

**+ Gradient** adds an editable colour rectangle behind your layers. Use it as a background, or raise it and try a blend mode over an image.

## Make the marks dance

In Move, open **Make the ink dance**. Ink tide pulses the tones, Little fizz shifts the grain, Passing cloud moves a tone wave across the page, and Check shuffle alternates neighbouring cells.

Pond rings sends circular tone waves out from the centre. Woven light crosses two waves over each other, like light falling through moving fabric.

They work with every style, including error diffusion and character art. Use a low amount to keep fine details steady. Cycles per loop repeats the texture movement within your chosen loop length.

Switching whole-print movement keeps a playing loop running. Choosing Still print pauses it. If you have layer motion or texture movement, press Play again to preview those on their own.

For a little movement in the picture itself, try **Lens breath**, a slow push in and out, or **Soft twist**, which gently turns the centre while leaving the edges steady. Mix either with a texture movement for a different feel.

## Six more ink sets

The Ink shelf now has 13 starting points. **Sunday poster** mixes blue and red with warm paper. **Seaside postcard** pairs petrol blue with coral. **Lilac notebook** is soft and dusty, **After the arcade** is brighter and greener, **Brown paper parcel** keeps things warm, and **Fern press** feels like a faded botanical print. Change any colour to make them your own.

## A slightly broken signal

The effects bench is in Print. Tape slip nudges horizontal bands. Block shuffle shifts blocks of the source. Wrong channel separates the source's colour channels before they are dithered. Tick the animation option to move these three effects through the loop.

Ink lantern spreads bright tones into their surroundings before printing, producing a dithered halo. Scanlines, dark corners and Negative pockets change the tones. All seven effects run through your chosen print style, keeping the output within your inks. Reset effects clears them together.

## A few shortcuts

Scroll over the page to zoom, press H for Pan, or drag with the middle mouse button. Press 0 to fit the page. In Layers, drag a corner to resize or the round handle to rotate; Shift snaps turns to 15°. New and the resize controls in Layers share Wobble's page sizes.

Space plays or pauses. R reverses inks. B switches before/after. Square brackets change mark size. Comma and full stop step through frames.

In Layers, arrows nudge your selection, Shift moves by ten, Ctrl/⌘+D duplicates and Delete removes. Ctrl/⌘+S saves; Ctrl/⌘+E exports. Themes and the full shortcut list live in **?**.

## A little atmosphere

In Layers, choose **+ Overlay** for stars, sparkles, lights or dust. Adjust their size, colour and scatter, then choose Twinkle, Slow pulse or Gentle drift and press Play. Use **Before dithering** to print them with your inks, or **After dithering** to keep their own colours above the print. Choose Still to stop just those marks.
