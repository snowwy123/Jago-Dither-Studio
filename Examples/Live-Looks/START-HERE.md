# Live looks

Open either `.jagodither` project in Dither 1.10 and press Play. The matching WebM is a compact video preview; the PNG shows one frame.

**Chrome Bloom Live:** slow sculpture breathing, subtle wave motion, rolling scanlines and a short phosphor echo. The sculpture has its own screen/glass/light settings; text layers keep their original colours.

**Acid Frequency Live:** optical contours with a subtle swirl, breathing artwork and drifting dither. Its typography stays clean and independently editable.

Projects are 720 pixels wide, three seconds, 12 fps. Preview videos are 480 pixels wide. Use Layers → select artwork → Edit layer look to change its effects. Use the main Look controls for following layers. Both loops use only original embedded art and local rendering.
