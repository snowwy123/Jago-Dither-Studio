# AFTER HOURS — four Dither looks

Open any `.jagodither` file using **Open** in Dither 1.9. The PNG next to it is the finished result at 1024 × 1280. `Showcase.png` compares all four. All artwork is original; the projects embed their sources and need no downloaded images.

- **Chrome Bloom:** reflected-colour sculpture with RGB slots, protected darkness, curved glass and restrained halation. Try Screen gap/columns, then Light's glow thresholds. Switch Lens off to compare the geometry.
- **Acid Frequency:** warped optical rings in acidic lime and dark green. Try other ordered dither sizes or switch to Halftone; compare the source-stage fisheye and optional grain texture.
- **Hot Press:** a sculptural flower form with elliptical screens, two active overlapping inks, small registration offsets and paper fibres. Colour holds the actual per-ink controls. Try a larger spacing or change the orange and mulberry inks.
- **Ghost Protocol:** a contour apparition rebuilt from measured-density characters. Try the character set, column count and glyph scale, or replace pale green with amber. Lens and scanlines add the terminal finish.

**The words are editable text layers**, including titles and footer lines. Open Layers, choose the text layer and edit its words, colour or position. The sculpture/optical/contour artwork is an embedded raster layer; its original construction is reproducible using the source repository's showcase-generation script. This is not an editable 3D model.

Use Original/Print to inspect the treatment. Effects remain separate saved settings, so bypass them or change the look rather than painting over the PNG. Start at 480 or 720 render resolution for quicker editing; these examples deliberately open at high detail. They are still poster projects, not animated presets.

All four saved projects were reopened in the actual studio and rendered again; the resulting pixels matched their original exports. No new app build or installer is required beyond the 1.9 preview.
