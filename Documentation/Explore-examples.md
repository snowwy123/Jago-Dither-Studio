# One home for examples

Open **Explore → Examples → Browse examples**. The gallery contains all 20 bundled examples, including the four AFTER HOURS posters, the two Live Looks, earlier technique projects and the original Wobble drawings.

Choose **Showcase** in Collection for Chrome Bloom, Acid Frequency, Hot Press, Ghost Protocol and their two live versions. Filter by Still or Moving, or search names and descriptions. Thumbnails are rendered from the actual studio results.

**Complete projects** include artwork, editable layers and their saved effects. **Source drawings** use the current look, so you can try your own treatment on the Wobble artwork. Opening an example replaces the working composition; Undo returns to your previous work. Save first if you want a separate file.

The examples are embedded in both the Windows app and offline HTML. You do not need to browse folders, extract an extra archive or download images. The individual files are still provided under Examples if you prefer them.

The other study buttons now lead here: **Look → Treat → Explore examples**, and **Move → Browse moving examples**. **Resources → Examples & studies** opens the same gallery too. None loads a different hidden study.

Explore also has **Looks & recipes**, which changes effects on your current artwork, and **Your directions**, for seeded comparisons and favourites. These are separate from complete example projects.

Moving projects open paused. Press Play to preview them. Live Looks demonstrate artwork with independent effects while editable lettering stays clean.
