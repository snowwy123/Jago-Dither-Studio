# Jago Dither

Turn pictures into prints, patterns and glowing screens.

Bring in an image or a short clip and explore halftones, layered inks, pixel patterns or screen effects. Compare treatments on the same artwork and keep the ones you want to come back to.

## Give it a try

Install with Jago Suite, or extract the portable download and open **Jago-Dither.exe**.

Add an image, choose a treatment and try it on your own colours. Use Explore directions when you want a few alternatives side by side.

[Find Jago Suite and downloads](https://jagosuite.co.uk/)
