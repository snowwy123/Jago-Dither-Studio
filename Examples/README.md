# A few good starting points

The four drawings under **Try it → Something to start with** are little Wobble animations. Try a different ink set, a texture movement, or your own lettering over the top.

Their editable originals are in **Wobble-originals**. Open a .jago file in Jago Wobble Studio to change its strokes, colours, layers or movement:

- **A quiet morning:** a striped mug, three curls of steam and a biscuit.
- **The wonky pear:** pencil shading and a leaf in a draught.
- **Make some noise:** brush letters with an uneven little sway.
- **Faraway hills:** pencil ridges, a warm sun and two birds.

Each starter in Dither is one 3-second loop layer. To bring over your changed drawing, export a PNG sequence ZIP from Wobble, then use **Layers → + Wobble loop** in Dither.

## A couple more experiments

Use Open in Dither for the .jagodither files, then press Play.

- **After hours:** editable gradient lettering, a colour wash, Ink lantern and texture fizz. Its GIF shows the print. Open **After-hours-ink-layers.jago** in Wobble to try the separated ink layers.
- **Your little tile.ditherrecipe:** a stair-step threshold tile and bright gradient inks. Import under Ink → Import recipe.
- **Bounce and print:** a Wobble ball loop with separate, editable lettering. Its .jago version is the flattened Dither print, ready to draw over in Wobble.

**Wobble-ball.zip** is a PNG sequence exported from Wobble. Choose New, then **Layers → + Wobble loop** to try importing it yourself.
